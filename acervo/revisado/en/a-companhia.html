Alupar is a holding company under private Brazilian control, founded in 2007, operating in the energy sector, more specifically in the transmission and generation segments, with the objective of developing and investing in projects in Brazil and in other Latin American countries. We are the largest 100% private company and one of the largest in the electricity transmission segment in Brazil, in terms of RAP.

In the transmission segment, Alupar holds the concession for 45 transmission systems, totaling more than 10,000 km of transmission lines, through 30-year concessions in Brazil and Peru, and lifetime concessions in Colombia and Chile.

In the energy generation segment and aligned with the sustainability of renewable sources, Alupar holds the concession/authorization of 16 generation projects, totaling nearly 800 MW of installed capacity, distributed across 4 UHEs, 4 PCHs, 7 wind farms and 1 photovoltaic plant.

The vast experience and results demonstrate Alupar’s technical competence, enabling the development of projects, whether in Energy Transmission or Generation, with the necessary efficiency and quality, from their conception to their effective operation, adding value to society and to the country, proven by the high quality levels of its operating projects.

In addition to the assets in Brazil, we are present in Chile through Transmissora de Energia de Santiago (TES) and SED; in Colombia through PCH Risaralda (Morro Azul), Transmissora Colombiana de Energia (TCE) and Transmissora de Energia de Los Llanos (TEL); and in Peru through the La Virgen Hydroelectric Plant, Transmissora Costa Norte (TCN), Transmissora de Energia de Puno (TEP), Transmissora Sierra Azul (TSA) and Transmissora de Energia de Runatullo (TER).

With a commitment to generating value for society and its shareholders, Alupar invests in technical competence, strong financial discipline and social responsibility to continue its sustainable growth through the development of generation projects and transmission systems.
