Alupar: energy that carries the present and helps transform the future

Alupar was created as a privately held Brazilian holding company and has become a multinational. Today it transmits, generates and trades electricity in Brazil, Chile, Colombia and Peru.

Founded in 2007, the company was born with a purpose that is simple to state and demanding to sustain for nearly two decades: to deliver energy with quality, safety and reliability, every day, to the people and businesses that do not always know it is there.

Alupar grows with financial discipline and genuine attention to social and environmental matters. In practice, that means more renewable sources entering the energy mix and more development in the regions where the company operates.

With a commitment to generating value for society and for its shareholders, Alupar invests in technical competence, strong financial discipline and social and environmental responsibility to continue its sustainable growth, through the development of highly complex projects.

And behind this growth are people. People who plan, build and operate with excellence, and who find in the company room to grow, to take on responsibility and to be the protagonists of their own work.

That is what drives Alupar: energy that carries the present and helps transform the future!

Mission and Vision

Mission

To transmit, generate and trade energy responsibly, with a view to economic development, social well-being, environmental preservation and the generation of value for shareholders.

Vision

To establish itself as a company of reference and excellence in the energy sector, operating with financial discipline, consistency and social and environmental responsibility in the regions where it operates.
